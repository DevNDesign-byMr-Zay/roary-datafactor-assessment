# Renewable AI Operations Roadmap

## Objective

Prepare the reference service architecture for future energy-aware workload decisions without weakening reliability or security boundaries.

## Priorities

### Workload intelligence

- Classify urgent versus flexible workloads.
- Preserve latency requirements for critical requests.
- Enable future renewable-preferred scheduling.

### Operational safety

Energy optimization must not:

- expose secrets,
- delay critical workloads incorrectly,
- bypass validation,
- reduce service reliability.

### Validation path

Future implementations should include:

- configuration tests,
- policy boundary tests,
- failure-mode coverage,
- operational documentation.
