# Renewable Workload Policy

## Objective

Provide a framework for shifting flexible workloads toward cleaner energy availability while preserving critical latency requirements.

## Policy concepts

- Critical workloads prioritize response time.
- Flexible workloads may prefer renewable availability windows.
- Decisions must remain observable and deterministic.

## Safety rules

- Never delay urgent workloads.
- Validate policy inputs.
- Keep execution decisions auditable.
